# Alder Cove — Game Doctrine

## Core Thesis
In an island ferry-dependent fishing town, children create notebooks, maps, symbols, and rituals to preserve each other from "Going Salt" — the town's slow erosion of memory, identity, and future selfhood.

## Emotional Core
Fear of false memories and sadness about growing up.

## Foundational Horror
The children recreate Alder Cove's emotional pathology while trying to protect each other from it.

## Central Phrase
Write it down before you forget.

## Going Salt
People begin Going Salt when:
- nobody reflects them back correctly anymore
- the town starts deciding who they are for them
- important memories go unwritten long enough to erode

Behaviorally, Going Salt appears as:
- memory drift
- withdrawal from future plans
- quiet narrative revision
- identity erosion

## Player Horror
The archivist gradually fears:
- they can no longer distinguish memory from documentation
- they are remembering differently than everyone else
- archiving itself may be a form of Going Salt

## Gameplay Core
Interpretive reconstruction gradually becomes participatory distortion.
