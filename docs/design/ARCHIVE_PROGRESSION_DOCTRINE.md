# Archive Progression Doctrine

The archive should escalate emotionally, not mechanically.

Progression is structured around:
- preservation instability
- emotional survivability
- archivist fatigue
- provenance uncertainty
- recursive handling

The player should gradually transition from:
- observing preserved memory
to:
- observing preservation itself destabilizing memory.

## Stage Structure

### Orientation
The archive introduces preservation philosophy and emotional framing.

### Softening
The player encounters emotionally softened copies and unstable wording.

### Partial Legibility
Fragments become partially readable through damaged preservation states.

### Preservation Conflict
The archive reveals ethically unstable preservation decisions and withheld readability.

### Archivist Instability
The archivist's recursive handling behavior becomes emotionally visible.

### Provenance Collapse
The archive can no longer confidently stabilize originals, copies, or handling order.
