# Alder Cove

Alder Cove is an island, ferry-dependent fishing town.

## Emotional Texture
Beautiful, isolated, intimate, weather-shaped, and quietly corrosive.

## Key Locations
- Ferry Dock
- Abandoned Cannery
- Flooded Lower Level
- Hidden Maintenance Tunnels
- Cliff Trail
- Safe Rocks
- Ferry Hill

## Cannery Myth
The cannery gives things back wrong sometimes.

People who stay below the waterline too long may come back changed.
